## Help us keep building free and open tools

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

Stoic Dreams is a small, independent studio. Much of what we build is free to use: the Web UI framework is open source, Ap Tul's browser tools cost nothing and keep your files on your device, and our software standards sites are free to read.

Sponsorship pays for the time it takes to build and maintain that work. It lets us spend more of our week on open source and free tools, and less on finding other ways to cover the bills.

</webui-page-segment>

## What your sponsorship supports

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- **[Web UI](https://webui.stoicdreams.com):** the open source web component framework behind every Stoic Dreams website.
- **[Ap Tul](https://www.aptul.com):** free, privacy-focused browser tools for images, PDFs, text, and development.
- **[Task Proxy](https://www.taskproxy.com):** our cross-platform developer tool for documentation, scripts, and automations.
- **Free guides and standards:** [Software Development Standards](https://www.softwarestandards.dev), [Software Engineer Standards](https://www.softwareengineerstandards.com), and our other [projects](/projects).

</webui-page-segment>

## How to sponsor us

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

We take sponsorships through GitHub Sponsors. You need a GitHub account, and you can sponsor as yourself or as an organization.

1. Go to our [GitHub Sponsors page](https://github.com/sponsors/StoicDreams).
2. Choose a monthly amount, or a one-time contribution.
3. Pick whether your sponsorship is shown publicly, then confirm.

GitHub handles the payment, and you can change or cancel a monthly sponsorship at any time from your GitHub account.
