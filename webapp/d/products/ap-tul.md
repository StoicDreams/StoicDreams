Ap Tul is a growing library of free, privacy-focused browser tools for images, PDFs, text, and development. Every tool exists because we needed it for a real project.

Many online tools upload your files to a server. Ap Tul's tools work in your browser, using Rust and WebAssembly where the work is heavy, so your files never leave your device. There are no paywalls and no clutter.
